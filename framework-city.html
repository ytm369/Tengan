Version 0 scope: price change, candle trend, order-book liquidity, BTC bias, relative strength, funding when fresh, candidate ranking, Gemini review, paper tracking, and guarded live-execution code. Chart-pattern validation, RSI/MACD/EMA/ADX, open interest, liquidations, autonomous strategy learning, and a live browser dashboard are not implemented analysis stages.

Why you may see “No fresh candidates” for a long time

Every review cycle is 60 seconds by default. A coin needs a ticker no older than 20 seconds, positive finite price, fresh directional node result(s), fresh liquidity score at least 0.15, and final strength at least 0.15. The earlier database audit found the ticker bootstrap did not continue updating; books alone cannot satisfy the fresh-ticker gate. With that feed condition, waiting longer will not fix the missing input.

Is the movement live?
